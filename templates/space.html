What a cell is not

The tracker does not always see every player in every frame. A cell's count is how often a player was placed there, so a quiet patch can mean nobody was there, or that nobody was seen there.

Goalies are in the counts. They sit in one cell for most of the game, which is a large share of a busy crease cell.

Position is the only thing a cell knows. It says nothing about facing direction, speed or whether the player was in the play.
