Theory
{# No panel around the text: the prose reads cleaner straight on the page, without a border competing with it. #}

Measuring defensive impact through events is difficult, because the most impactful defense results in events not happening. For instance passes not completed or not even attempted. Good defense is typically shown in the lag of dangerous plays against.

Analytics Perspective

In analytics you will typically define defense as the ability to prevent shots and chances against. Statistically, this makes perfect sense and it's very easy to define and interpret. However, you can prevent shots against in many different ways. You can excel at playing without the puck or you can excel at keeping the puck for long periods of time. Possessing the puck in the offensive zone while playing with low risk is a great way to prevent chances against.

Quite often you will see 3rd and 4th liners top the defensive metrics, because they excel at low risk cycling the puck in the offensive end.

This isn't necessarily a wrong definition of defense - It's just different from how most coaches and players define defense.

Coaching Perspective

Most coaches and players will define defense as what you do when the opposing team has possession of the puck. Likewise, offense is what you do when your team is in possession of the puck. At least this has always been my way of thinking about offense vs. defense.

This is a different perspective than the analytical one, since we're now focusing only on the time where the opponent team has the puck.

The difference of definitions can lead to misunderstandings. If a coach thinks a defense metric means one thing, but in reality it means something else then it can cause problems. This is also why a defense metric leaderboard might not show what you expect from the eye test.

Primary and Secondary defenders

In this project the focus will be on the coaching perspective. How impactful are the players when playing without the puck in their own zone. To do this we will use the Big Data Cup 2026 tracking data to define some new defensive metrics, but first we need to define the concepts of primary and secondary defenders.

In this project I'm defining the primary defender(s) as the defending player closest to the puck or any defending players within 6 feet of the puck. This means that you can have more than one primary defender, but there's typically just one primary defender. The other defending players are then categorized as secondary defenders.

Being a good primary defender is often what you see and what shows up in event data - Winning puck battles, blocking shots, preventing zone entries etc.

Good secondary defense rarely shows up in the spreadsheet or gets noticed at all. Good secondary defense is preventing plays from happening at all (pass interceptions being the exception). In other words, it shows up as the lack of dangerous plays.

Since you typically have one primary defender and four secondary defenders, it's fair to say that good secondary defense is much more important than good primary defense. This also illustrates the issue of relying solely on micro statistics to measure defensive impact - Most good defensive plays don't show up in the event data.

We understand that defensive events like shot blocks and even takeaways are a pretty bad measurement for defensive impact. That's why we often rely on macro statistics like xGA to estimate defensive impact. Here we will try and take a different approach than looking at events or even macro statistics.

Analysis through times and distances

Much of the analysis in this project will revolve around time measurements and distances, so let's define some new defensive metrics.

Time metrics

Time_dz_primary

Time playing as the primary defender in the defensive zone. This is both a measurement of the player's skills as primary and secondary defender. If you're a good and aggressive primary defender you can quickly force the puck carrier to play the puck or even force a turnover. If you're a good secondary defender you will rarely become the primary defender in the first place because your coverage is great.

Time_dz_def

The total time playing defense in the defensive zone. This is more of a team/macro statistic.

Percentage_primary

What percentage of the total defensive time does the player spend as the primary defender? You would expect the best defenders to play the least as primary defenders. Firstly, because they are aggressive as primary defenders. Secondly, because they provide good coverage as secondary defenders. Lastly, because the opposing team (consciously or unconsciously) will play away from the best defenders.

Distance metrics

Dist_net_dz_def

The average player distance to the net when playing defense in the defensive zone. This can tell something about how passive/compact vs. aggressive the defense is. The closer the players are to their own net the more passive the defensive structure is.

Dist_net_primary

The average player distance to the net when playing as the primary defender in your own zone. This tells us two things - How aggressive is the player and how good is the player at keeping the play to the outside (away from the slot)?

Puck_dist_net_primary

The average puck distance to the net while playing as the primary defender. This is just a cleaner measurement of the above. We're now looking at puck placement instead of player placement.

Dist_puck_primary

How close is the primary defender to the puck - How aggressive is the primary defender?

Dist_puck_secondary

How close is the puck when playing as a secondary defender. Generally, it's a good sign if the puck is far away from you, but it could also be a sign of forwards not helping out deep enough in the zone.

Event metrics

Lastly, we can look specifically at the shots that happen while the player is the primary defender - Responsible for the puck carrier.

Blocked_primary

The number of blocked shots while the player is the primary defender.

Missed_primary

The number of missed shots while the player is the primary defender.

On_net_primary

The number of shots on net while the player is the primary defender.

Goal_primary

The number of goals while the player is the primary defender.
