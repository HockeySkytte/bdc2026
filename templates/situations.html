Strength is written from the home team's point of view, so 5v4 is a power play for {{ meta.home_team }} and 4v5 is one for {{ meta.away_team }}. The bar is {{ meta.home_team }}'s share of the frames where somebody held the puck at that strength.

What the numbers say

Possession follows the power play, which is a sanity check on the whole pipeline: the team with the extra skater holds the puck far more often than not, and the short-handed side is well under half.

Five on five sits near even, because it is the state both teams spend most of the game in.
