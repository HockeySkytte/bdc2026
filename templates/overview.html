What the tracking file is

Every frame is one still of the game, and each row in it is one player or the puck, with a position in feet from the middle of the rink. There is no event in this data, and no shot count: it is where everybody was, thirty times a second.

That makes the interesting questions spatial. Who is holding the puck, how far away everybody else is, and which half of the ice the play is in.

{{ "{:,}".format(meta.dropped_rows) }} rows in the raw files had no game clock and could not be placed in time, so they are left out of these numbers.
